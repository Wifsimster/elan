Élan tracks your cycling, running and walking outings and your strength workouts, with no account and no cloud. Everything stays on your phone.

🚴 GPS OUTINGS (cycling, running, walking)
• Reliable GPS recording, even with the screen off
• Distance, speed or pace, elevation gain, moving time
• Bluetooth heart-rate strap and cadence/speed sensor, automatic reconnection
• Time in each heart-rate zone, calories and effort level
• Personal records and speed, altitude and heart-rate charts
• Route on a map (optional online basemap) and GPX export for Strava

🏋️ STRENGTH TRAINING
• Ready-made programs and a customizable weekly plan
• Catalog of 40+ illustrated exercises with suggested loads and reps for your goal
• Sets, loads, rest timer, estimated 1RM
• Per-exercise effort rating and automatic weekly program progression
• Pause a workout and resume it whenever you want

📈 PROGRESS
• Full history with search and filters
• Weekly summary, goals (sessions, distance, tonnage)
• Body-weight journal and workout reminders
• Share a session as an image

🔒 YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No account, no ads, no trackers
• 100% offline by default: your data never leaves your phone
• Import Strava files (GPX, TCX, FIT)
• Optional backup to your own S3 server, configurable with a QR code
• Optional export to Health Connect and a Markdown/JSON report for your coach

Note: calories are estimates (MET method), not a medical measurement.
